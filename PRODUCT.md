# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS in a single `index.html` (canvas 2D for the world, HTML overlays for cards and HUD), no build step, no external libraries. Hosted on GitHub Pages (`joacocastillo11.github.io/juego-biosensor`). Proposed by Claude and accepted by the author without objection.

## Users

- **Teaching assistants (ayudantes)** of UC course EAA2072 grading the game alone, on a computer, after the Canvas deadline (2026-11-24 23:59). They play it up to twice and read the Ficha.
- **Two classmates** playing it during the in-class presentation (2026-11-25), likely on phones or the professor's computer, with a 5-minute window.
- General public: people who use a phone or computer daily but have no technical training in biosensors.

## Product Purpose

A playable piece of science communication (producción creativa) for the author, Joaquín Castillo, that in at most 5 minutes start to finish:
1. explains what autonomous biochemical sensing is (WEF & Frontiers, Top 10 Emerging Technologies 2025);
2. explains the real application analysed in his Informe Previo: the Nix hydration biosensor (sweat patch) and how it works in context;
3. communicates the author's critical position on one consequence (dimension: Responsible AI, supervising what the algorithm estimates; autonomy vs. dependence).

Success: a person with no technical background finishes the run, can say what the sensor measures and how, and can repeat the author's stance: "decide with the data, not by the data."

## Positioning

The game's mechanic is the argument: the sensor readout is shown with its real error margin and can drop out (river crossing: patch not water-resistant; app disconnect: Android failures in the ECU 2026 study). The player wins by combining the sensor's estimate with the body's own signals, and the ending reveals real vs. estimated values and what kind of decision-maker the player was.

## Operating Context

Graded with the course rubric: clarity for the general public, author's critical position, understandable within 5 minutes, creativity (a deliberate design decision), AI-use transparency, overall perception (connect course concepts). Played from a link with no install. Must also work on a projector in class.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 3-lane runner on a mountain bike through Torres del Paine, Chile; move left/right (keyboard + swipe/tap).
- Three indicators confirmed by the author: hidratación, energía, fatiga. Each has a consumable pickup on the track. The technology is presented in general, not only Nix: hydration from sweat (Nix, available since 2022, mean error 17.2%), energy from glucose (continuous glucose monitors such as Dexcom; UC San Diego experimental microneedle patch), fatigue from lactate (UC San Diego experimental patch).
- If an indicator gets critical the rider slows, then dismounts and walks the bike; there is a time target to reach the finish.
- Events confirmed: river crossing (sensor without signal) and app disconnected.
- Run must end within 5 minutes total, intro and ending included.
- Spanish (Chile) only.

## Brand Commitments

- Aesthetic pinned by the author: drawing and animation inspired by a 1990s jazz-noir space-western anime (the author named Cowboy Bebop as inspiration only). Inspiration, not imitation: no characters, logos, title, or catchphrases from that show.
- Runner genre inspired by 3-lane endless runners; no names, art, or characters from existing games.
- Setting: Torres del Paine, Chile; mountain biking.

## Evidence on Hand

All facts come from the author's Informe Previo (submitted 2026-11-15): Nix launch 2022-12-07 (Boston), bicep patch with sweat channel and two electrode pairs, temperature-corrected algorithms, 17.2% mean error on treadmill, recreational use only, not water-resistant, USD 189 kit / USD 25 per 4 patches, ECU 2026 study (data recovered for 24 of 30 participants due to Android app issues), 2025 conference abstract with low agreement between sweat tools, UCSD 2022 microneedle patch (glucose + lactate or alcohol), Dexcom G7 / WISDM trial. No other claims may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The mechanic carries the message; text supports it.
2. Every number on screen is traceable to the Informe Previo.
3. The author's voice owns the stance; Claude drafts, the author corrects.
4. Finishable by anyone in under 5 minutes, on desktop or phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable on a classroom projector and on a phone; keyboard and touch controls; sound optional with a visible mute; respects reduced motion where possible.
